# MedGuard AI Frontend v1.2

## Added
- Fully interactive owner-configurable AI Zone Editor.
- Click-to-draw polygon, rectangle, entry/exit line and calibration reference points.
- Multiple zones per camera and Ignore Zone type.
- Zone presets, schedules, sensitivity, delay, recording and per-zone rule selection.
- Zone links to inventory shelf/rack, POS counter, cash drawer and shift context.
- Test Selected Zone workflow and saved zone configuration in browser localStorage.
- Rule simulation with cooldown and confidence settings.
- Human review feedback and incident-case workflow.
- Shift-level cash reconciliation.
- Inventory rack-to-camera zone mapping and high-risk medicine configuration.
- Event confidence alongside risk score.

## Important
This is still a frontend prototype. No real CCTV, POS, UPI/card, payment, inventory or AI backend is connected.
